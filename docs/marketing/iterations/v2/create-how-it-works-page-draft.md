## Stop Losing the Ideas You Find While Reading

You clip the article. You forget the insight. Three weeks later you're trying to remember where you read it.

Reader is the read-it-later app that turns passive reading into durable knowledge — through a distraction-free reading mode, anchored notes, and an AI session review that synthesises what you actually retained.

---

## How It Works in Three Steps

### Step 1 — Save Anything, Instantly

Use the **Chrome extension** to push any article, blog post, or PDF to your library with one click. Reader strips ads, sidebars, and tracking scripts, leaving only the content.

- Web articles → clean reader view
- PDFs → native PDF viewer with the same annotation tools
- Link-only saves → jump straight to the source with one tap

No app-switching. No copy-pasting URLs. Just save and keep reading.

---

### Step 2 — Read and Drop Notes Right on the Page

Open any saved item in Reader's reader view. As you read, press the note button to anchor a note to the exact paragraph you're reacting to.

- Notes are **pinned to a specific location** in the article — not floating in a separate notebook
- Each note shows a preview of the text it's anchored to, so context is never lost
- Edit or refine notes later without losing your place
- Works identically for PDFs

When you're done, your annotations live with the article — not scattered across apps.

---

### Step 3 — Let AI Summarise Your Reading Session

After a reading session, trigger **Session Review**. Reader's AI looks at the article and your notes together and generates two things:

1. **What you read** — a concise summary of the article's argument
2. **Your focus** — a synthesis of what your notes say about where your attention went

This turns a reading session into a reference you can actually use. No re-reading. No hunting through notes apps.
