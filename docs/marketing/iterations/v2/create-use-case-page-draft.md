# Use Cases for Reader: Read-it-Later, Annotation, and Note Capture

This document outlines five core use cases for the Reader application, focusing on its read-it-later, annotation, and note-taking capabilities.

---

### Use Case 1: The Lifelong Learner

- **User:** A student, academic, or any individual dedicated to self-study and knowledge acquisition.
- **Job to be Done:** To collect, comprehend, and synthesize information from a wide array of digital sources to build a deep and interconnected understanding of a subject.
- **Example:** A university student is working on a thesis about renewable energy. They save articles from academic journals, news websites, and industry blogs throughout their research process. Using Reader, they highlight key statistics and arguments, add notes to connect concepts from different papers, and tag everything under "Thesis Research" to create a centralized, annotated library for their project.
- **CTA:** Stop juggling tabs and losing track of valuable information. Start building your personal knowledge library today with Reader.

---

### Use Case 2: The Knowledge Worker

- **User:** A professional, such as a software engineer, market analyst, designer, or product manager.
- **Job to be Done:** To efficiently stay informed about industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices without disrupting their primary workflow.
- **Example:** A UX designer saves articles about new design patterns and accessibility standards as they come across them during the week. On Friday afternoon, they dedicate an hour to go through their saved articles in Reader. They highlight inspiring examples, annotate screenshots with their own ideas, and build a "swipe file" of ideas to bring to their next team meeting.
- **CTA:** Turn information overload into a competitive advantage. Supercharge your professional development and stay ahead of the curve.

---

### Use Case 3: The Curious Hobbyist

- **User:** An enthusiast passionate about a specific hobby, such as cooking, gardening, woodworking, or programming.
- **Job to be Done:** To collect, organize, and personalize information, tutorials, and project ideas for future reference and execution.
- **Example:** A home chef discovers recipes on various food blogs and cooking websites. They save their favorites to Reader and use the annotation feature to note their own modifications, such as ingredient substitutions or adjusted cooking times. When they're ready to cook, they have a personalized, tested recipe ready to go.
- **CTA:** From inspiration to execution. Organize your hobbies, projects, and passions in one clean, accessible space.

---

### Use Case 4: The Content Creator

- **User:** A blogger, journalist, newsletter writer, or social media manager.
- **Job to be Done:** To gather, curate, and process source material and inspiration for their next piece of content.
- **Example:** A writer for a popular tech newsletter saves articles, studies, and Twitter threads that catch their eye. They highlight surprising statistics and compelling quotes, adding notes with their own analysis or counter-arguments. When it's time to write, they have a pre-vetted and annotated collection of resources to quickly outline their next issue.
- **CTA:** Fuel your next great story. Streamline your research and never let a good idea slip away.

---

### Use Case 5: The Collaborative Team

- **User:** A small team working on a shared research or discovery project.
- **Job to be Done:** To share, discuss, and build a collective understanding of relevant articles, research papers, and competitor analyses.
- **Example:** A product marketing team is researching a new competitor. As team members find articles, press releases, and reviews, they save them to a shared space in Reader. They highlight the competitor's value propositions and customer feedback, leaving notes and questions for their colleagues. This creates a centralized intelligence hub that informs their strategic response.
- **CTA:** Stop emailing links. Make your team's research collaborative, efficient, and actionable.
