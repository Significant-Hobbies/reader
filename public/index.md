## A saved tab is *only an intention.*

How it works

Reader keeps the list small: what you saved, whether you have read it, and a way back to it.

### Save it

Paste a URL in the inbox, or click Save link in the Chrome extension. Reader stores the title, the address and the date.

### Find it

Switch between unread, read and all. Search matches titles and addresses.

### Mark it

Links stay unread until you say otherwise. Opening or fetching a link never marks it read on its own.

## Ask about what you saved, *in the conversation you are already in.*

With ChatGPT

Reader connects to ChatGPT through MCP with OAuth. Reading and discussion happen there; Reader holds the list.

### Search

“What have I saved about databases?” finds matching links in your inbox.

### Fetch

Pulls the available text of a link. If a site blocks extraction, the original address is still there.

### Mark read

Marks a link read, or unread again, only when you ask. Read and write are separate permissions.

## Small on purpose, *and private to your account.*

Boundaries

Reader is a maintained personal tool. It does one job and does not grow past it.

### Google sign-in

Your inbox belongs to your account and is isolated from every other.

### Chrome extension

Saves the active tab's address and title. Built from source for a local unpacked install.

### Not in Reader

No RSS, annotations, boards, PDF capture or built-in AI. Earlier saves are kept and appear as links.

### No checkout

There is no public plan or payment.

## Before you *save a link.*





### How do I add a link?

Paste a URL in the inbox, or click Save link in the Chrome extension.

### What does ChatGPT see?

Only what its tools ask for: a search over your saved titles and addresses, the text of a link you point it at, and the read state of a link you ask it to change.

### Where did the other features go?

RSS, the reader view, annotations, boards, memories, PDFs and built-in AI are outside the active product. Existing saved data is retained.

### Can I install the Chrome extension?

It is built from the repository and loaded unpacked in Chrome. Public store distribution is deferred.
